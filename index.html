THE SHORT VERSION

HOW TO CLIMB

SWING THE POLE

The tip of the pole chases your cursor. It has weight, momentum, and opinions. Sweep the mouse to build a swing.

LEVER, DON'T LEAP

Hook the tip over a ledge, then pull the cursor down. The pole pries you upward. Hold left mouse to grip harder.

THROW THE HOOK

Right mouse fires the hook toward the cursor. It bites any rock or timber within reach, then reels you in while held.

FALL. RETURN. CLIMB.

There is no death, only inconvenience. R returns you to the last checkpoint flag. The sea returns you there too, with less dignity.

TIP — The pole reaches furthest when it is nearly perpendicular to the cliff. Get close. Commit. Regret later.
